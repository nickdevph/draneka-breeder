## What becomes canonical

The promoted v0.4 authority includes the v0.3 lineage guarantees plus the independently qualified v0.4 operating model and bounded corrections:

- Program is lightweight durable breeding context, not mandatory setup ceremony.
- Event-first Program creation is atomic: cancelling the initiating biological record leaves no ghost Program.
- Known-pair Betta ancestry remains exact where evidence exists.
- Medaka supports multiple independent egg batches, explicit source selection, partial hatch, and repeat hatch observations from one source batch.
- Neocaridina and other group/population-derived stock preserve population provenance without fabricated exact parents or forced pair semantics.
- Offspring provenance can retain source output and source hatch identity through downstream operations.
- Tanks remain locations/environmental context rather than lineage owners.
- Suggestion, scheduled action, completed action, observation, and derived state remain semantically distinct.
- The routine product surface uses breeder-native language and retains anti-overwhelm constraints.
