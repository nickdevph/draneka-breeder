## Product changes admitted by v0.3

v0.3 retains holistic v0.2 and locks in the lineage expansion demonstrated in the lineage candidate:

- automatic lineage derived from reproductive events;
- cohorts as lineage-bearing objects;
- cohort splits preserve ancestry and do not create generations;
- selected/promoted breeders inherit recorded cohort/spawn ancestry;
- Pair Builder relatedness checks;
- common-ancestor relationship paths;
- pedigree/ancestry coverage language;
- `No known common ancestor` rather than claiming unrelatedness when ancestry is incomplete;
- explicit unknown parentage and group-spawn parentage;
- full web-oriented lineage graph plus compact lineage views;
- no genotype inference from phenotype.

Canonical lineage loop:

`Spawn -> Cohort -> Selection -> Breeding Stock -> Pairing Check -> Next Reproductive Event`

Only a reproductive event advances ancestry/generation. Splits, moves, transfers, sales, and selection do not.
