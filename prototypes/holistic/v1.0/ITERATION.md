## Primary product question

Can the holistic breeder experience become simpler and implementation-admissible without weakening canonical v0.9 biological, operational, selection, provenance or commerce authority?

## Allowed v1.0 scope

This is a **simplify-and-qualify** iteration, not a broad feature iteration.

Allowed work:

- remove redundant navigation/surfaces;
- improve discoverability of existing capabilities;
- reduce or clearly expose tap/step cost for frequent tasks;
- strengthen empty/error/recovery states;
- improve accessibility affordances;
- clarify mobile/web parity boundaries;
- normalize terminology;
- prepare independent breeder usability review paths;
- prepare production implementation-admission evidence.

## Explicit non-scope

Do not introduce new biological authority or new product domains. In particular, v1.0 does not authorize:

- new reproductive semantics;
- new ancestry/generation inference;
- genotype inference;
- automatic best-pair authority;
- marketplace/channel management in Breeder;
- pricing/CRM/payment/order/shipping/finance workflows;
- broad capacity forecasting or live-food ERP;
- backend/API/schema/database changes;
- Android/web implementation;
- deployment, production, signing or release.

## Required authority preservation

Any v1.0 candidate must preserve, at minimum:

- v0.9 public/private evidence projection, singular commerce quantity ownership, reconciliation separation, and Sale/rehome breeding boundary;
- v0.8 operational attention and matching causal-resolution semantics;
- v0.7 selection/disposition, Pair Builder, goal history, phenotype/genotype and generation-evidence boundaries;
- v0.6 species/provenance uncertainty, including population-derived and annual-killifish semantics;
- v0.5 count, mortality, move, split, merge, stage, feeding and hatch/output operation distinctions.

## Qualification requirement

The Founder/producer lane must not act as the independent reviewer for this iteration.

A separate reviewer must independently bind the exact Drive bytes and PR producer head, execute the usability/accessibility/recovery and canonical-regression gates, and return an independent disposition before any Founder promotion or merge.
