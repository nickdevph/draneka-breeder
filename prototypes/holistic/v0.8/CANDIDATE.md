## v0.8 bounded product delta

The candidate implements the Founder-locked `Operational scale` question from `prototypes/holistic/v0.8/ITERATION.md`:

1. Dynamic Breeder Round sourced from explicit one-off plans, recurring husbandry, lifecycle attention, capacity context, grow-out plans and critical dependencies.
2. Explainable attention items with visible source, reason and operational state.
3. Recurring schedule completion / defer / skip / future-cadence edit with append-only occurrence history semantics.
4. Lifecycle prompts that remain recommendations/checks until the breeder records an actual biological or husbandry event.
5. Breeder-entered capacity context where unknown remains unknown and known thresholds are planning aids only.
6. Grow-out planning that remains distinct from the actual cohort move/split mutation.
7. Explicit live-food/culture dependency state where maintenance/availability never fabricates feeding.
8. Preservation of canonical v0.7 selection/line-development authority and v0.6/v0.5 provenance/operations behavior.

## Important semantic boundaries

- Due does not mean done.
- Suggested does not mean observed.
- A schedule completion is an operational completion record and does not substitute for a detailed feeding, biological or cohort-mutation record.
- A lifecycle prompt does not create eggs, hatch, wetting, sex, selection, parent removal, move or split.
- A grow-out plan does not mutate cohort quantity/location and is marked executed only after a real canonical cohort mutation is saved.
- Capacity context is breeder-entered planning context, not an authoritative stocking recommendation.
- Culture state/maintenance does not create a feeding event.
- Operational state does not rewrite lineage, reproductive-output, hatch, wetting-attempt, phenotype-evaluation, selection-history or generation evidence.
