# 4. Mandatory v0.8 gates

## G1 — Dynamic Breeder Round authority

Establish independently that Breeder Round is dynamically derived from current operational evidence rather than merely displaying a static predefined demo list.

Exercise enough state changes to prove that:

- explicit one-off plans can surface;
- recurring schedules can surface;
- lifecycle prompts can surface;
- overdue/exception state changes ordering/elevation;
- capacity context can surface when meaningful;
- critical culture/dependency state can surface;
- resolving/defering/changing the source changes the round appropriately;
- every active item exposes why it exists and what record/context it concerns.

A generic inbox with untraceable items is a promotion blocker.

## G2 — Due does not mean done

For recurring schedules, independently exercise:

1. a due occurrence;
2. an overdue occurrence;
3. explicit completion;
4. defer;
5. skip;
6. future cadence edit.

Expected:

- becoming due creates no completion event by itself;
- explicit completion appends operational history;
- defer retains the original due occurrence/history;
- skip does not fabricate husbandry/biology;
- schedule edits do not rewrite previous completion/defer/skip history;
- the next due value follows the explicit cadence decision.

## G3 — Schedule completion vs detailed husbandry records

Use the seeded Betta recurring feeding schedule.

Expected:

- simply becoming due creates no feeding event;
- recording schedule completion is visibly an operational completion record;
- it must not silently substitute a false detailed feeding payload or invent food/portion data;
- the canonical detailed feeding workflow must remain available and separate.

## G4 — Lifecycle suggestion vs biological fact

Exercise lifecycle prompts across materially different species.

Mandatory:

### Annual killifish

- a wetting/diapause review becoming due creates **no wetting attempt and no hatch**;
- marking the review checked creates no wetting/hatch;
- an explicit wetting action creates the attempt;
- re-drying remains attached to that attempt;
- a later wetting remains a distinct attempt;
- only explicit observed hatch creates offspring and links to the correct attempt.

### Medaka

- a hatch-review prompt creates no hatch;
- marking checked creates no hatch;
- an explicit hatch remains its own provenance-bearing record.

### Betta / guppy / Apistogramma where applicable

- grow-out/sexing/parental-care prompts do not invent split, sex, selection, parent-removal or other outcomes.

Any timing-based fabricated biology is a BLOCKER.

## G5 — Capacity unknown/known semantics

Exercise at least one context with no planning ceiling and one with a breeder-entered planning ceiling.

Expected:

- unknown stays explicitly unknown;
- Draneka does not invent a stocking limit;
- a known pressure derives from recorded cohort quantities + breeder-entered context;
- the UI does not present the ceiling as a biological truth or authoritative stocking recommendation;
- no automatic cull, sale, rehome, split, move or pairing action occurs.

## G6 — Grow-out plan vs real mutation

Use an active cohort with a grow-out plan.

Expected:

- creating/editing a plan does not change cohort count, tank, active state, identity or provenance;
- the plan clearly says it is a plan;
- only a canonical explicit move/split changes the cohort;
- split quantity is conserved;
- provenance survives;
- the plan becomes executed only after the real operation actually occurs;
- cancelling a plan must not mutate cohort reality.

## G7 — Critical live-food/culture dependency

Exercise a breeder-recorded culture dependency.

Expected:

- dependency exists because it is explicitly recorded/relevant, not because the system assumes a universal food requirement;
- at-risk/low state elevates attention;
- culture maintenance/status updates append history;
- changing culture availability does not create feeding;
- resolving culture maintenance does not silently mark linked fry husbandry complete.

- source class;
- reason;
- Program/cohort/tank/resource context;
- due/window where established;
- current operational state;
- history/action that closed, deferred, skipped or changed it.

Historical reasons must survive future schedule edits.

## G9 — Anti-overwhelm / product-shape discipline

Expected:

- no Kanban/Gantt/general ERP surface appears;
