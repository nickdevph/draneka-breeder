## v0.7 product proof

### Breeding goal as durable context

A Program carries a current plain-language breeding goal. Goal edits create history rather than rewriting past selection evidence. Every evaluation and selection session retains its own goal snapshot so a later goal edit cannot change why an earlier decision was made.

### Time-stamped phenotype evaluation

The candidate adds contextual phenotype/trait evaluations for breeder stock/candidates and cohorts. Each evaluation records a timestamp, observed phenotype/traits, breeder assessment, notes, optional cohort sample size, and attached evidence references.

The semantic boundary is explicit: an observed phenotype and breeder assessment are evidence records; neither proves genotype.

### Photo/evidence attachment

Evaluation evidence can include an explicitly attached photo. The prototype uses clearly labelled synthetic demo evidence where seeded examples require visible evidence. Missing evidence remains missing and is surfaced as such.

### Selection sessions

A Program-scoped selection session compares recorded candidates against the current breeding goal and their recorded evidence. Each decision requires a breeder reason and stores the goal snapshot used at that time.

Admitted decisions are:

- `Holdback`;
- `Non-breeding`;
- `Sale / rehome`;
- `Retire`;
- `Undecided`.

These are breeder decisions, not inferred biological states.

### Holdback and disposition boundaries

A selected animal may become a breeder holdback/candidate while retaining all existing lineage/provenance. Holdback does not infer breeding readiness, genotype, future breeding success, or a recommended mate.

`Sale / rehome` in v0.7 is a disposition label only. It creates no marketplace listing, channel allocation, commerce quantity, or sales workflow.

### Generation/cohort comparison

A Program can compare recorded evaluation outcomes across cohorts/generations against the breeding goal. The comparison uses only recorded evidence. Uneven/missing evidence is explicitly labelled rather than converted into a neutral or zero score.

### Pair Builder authority retained

Existing lineage/relatedness checks remain in Pair Builder. The candidate preserves sibling/relationship warnings and conservative behavior when ancestry evidence is incomplete. It explicitly declines opaque authoritative `best pair` selection.

## Required stress paths represented

- **Betta:** exact controlled ancestry, multiple phenotype evaluations, evidence, selection session, holdback/disposition, parentage unchanged.
- **Fancy guppy:** visible phenotype selection while sire uncertainty remains unresolved.
- **Medaka:** multi-cohort/generation comparison while distinct hatch/source provenance and missing-evidence handling remain visible.
- **Neocaridina:** population/colony-derived selection and holdback without fabricated exact parents; Pair Builder remains conservative/ineligible when evidence is insufficient.
- **v0.6 killifish regression:** mop/plant collection and annual wetting/re-drying/second-wetting/explicit-hatch provenance remain intact.

## UX/scope discipline

- No new top-level navigation destination was introduced.
- Selection and evaluation enter contextually from Program, stock/candidate, cohort/grow-out, analytics/media, and lineage-related surfaces.
- Frequent evaluation actions remain compact/mobile-first with progressive detail.
- Comparison prioritizes goal-linked evidence rather than becoming a generic analytics dashboard.
- `Observed`, `Breeder assessment`, `Selection decision`, and `Unknown` remain semantically distinct.

## Explicitly not admitted

This candidate does not introduce:

- genotype prediction from phenotype;
- automated/opaque `best pair` authority;
- broad genetics calculators;
- v0.8 dynamic Breeder Round scheduling/attention expansion;
- capacity forecasting/operational allocation planning;
- v0.9 commerce handoff, marketplace listing, channel allocation, or sales CRM;
- backend/API/schema/database/deployment/production mutation;
- Android or web implementation authority.
