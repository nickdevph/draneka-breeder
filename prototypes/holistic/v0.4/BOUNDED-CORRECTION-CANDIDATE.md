## Bounded corrections embodied

### V04-C01 — Atomic event-first creation

Starting from `Record a spawn`, `Collect eggs`, or `Observe new juveniles` now creates a draft lightweight Program context. The Program and its initiating biological record are committed together only when the biological record is saved. Cancelling the second step leaves no ghost Program.

### V04-C02 — Neutral population-derived stock semantics

Population/group-derived selected stock no longer receives `Derived from recorded spawn` or forced Pair Builder framing. Exact parent IDs remain absent when unknown. Source-population provenance is retained, and generation language remains evidence-limited.

### V04-C03 — Repeatable hatch/provenance foundation

Egg outputs now support repeatable hatch observations rather than one terminal scalar hatch. One egg batch can produce multiple offspring groups through distinct hatch records. Offspring groups, splits, and selected stock retain explicit source-output and source-hatch provenance arrays while legacy source fields remain available for prototype compatibility. This establishes the v0.5-safe provenance foundation without implementing the full v0.5 split/merge UX.

### V04-C04 — Explicit Medaka hatch-source selection

When exactly one egg batch is open, direct hatch entry remains available. When more than one batch is open, the Program-level `Record a hatch` action requires explicit batch selection before the biological record form opens.

### V04-C05 — Breeder-native default information surface

Default working surfaces no longer foreground architecture-proof terminology such as `Program = context`, `Parentage context`, `Reproductive output`, or an always-visible `Evidence semantics` glossary. Breeder-native labels such as `Egg collections` and `Breeding stock & source` are primary; detailed record semantics remain available through contextual help.

## Locked behavior preserved

- Program remains lightweight durable context rather than mandatory setup ceremony.
- Betta known-pair → spawn → offspring remains straightforward.
- Multiple Medaka egg batches coexist independently.
- Neocaridina population/colony provenance does not fabricate sire/dam.
- Completing a check does not manufacture eggs, hatch, juveniles, or offspring facts.
- Tanks remain locations rather than lineage owners.
- Moves and splits preserve ancestry/provenance.
- Incomplete ancestry remains incomplete.
- Phenotype and genotype remain separate.
- No marketplace ERP, finance/CRM, predictive genetics, broad analytics, hardware, social, or individual-fry scope expansion is admitted.
