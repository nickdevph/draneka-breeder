## Bounded product question

Can a breeder maintain a lightweight stable breeding context across parentage, reproductive output and offspring without being forced into tank-centric or pair/spawn-centric assumptions?

## Corrections embodied in this candidate

1. `Breeding Program` is a durable organizing context, not mandatory setup ceremony.
2. The neutral conceptual core is:
   `Program context -> Parentage context -> Reproductive output/event -> Offspring group -> Selection`.
3. The same core is stress-tested with:
   - Betta splendens — discrete known-pair/spawn path;
   - Oryzias latipes / Medaka — repeated egg-collection batches inside one Program;
   - Neocaridina davidi — breeding-population provenance with exact parentage unknown.
4. The product distinguishes:
   `Suggestion -> Scheduled action -> Completed action -> Observation -> Derived state`.
5. Completing or checking a task does not fabricate a biological observation.
6. Repeated Medaka collections remain separate reproductive-output records without requiring a new Program for each collection.
7. A selected non-current Medaka egg batch can hatch into its own offspring group while other open egg batches remain open.
8. Group/population provenance does not become exact sire/dam by inference.
9. Tanks remain current locations/environmental context and do not own ancestry.
10. Marketplace/commerce management remains outside Breeder.

## Explicitly not in v0.4

- full species-overlay framework;
- predictive genetics;
- automatic best-pair authority;
- marketplace publishing or marketplace management;
- finance / CRM;
- broad analytics expansion;
- individual records for every fry/shrimplet;
- broad capacity forecasting;
- live-food inventory ERP;
- social/community features;
- hardware integrations.
